The craft

Three things we
won't compromise on.

Made by a person

Hand-embroidered, hand-glazed, hand-finished. You can see the maker's decisions in the piece — the slight asymmetry is the signature, not a defect.

Small numbers

We buy in runs of a few, sometimes one. It keeps the floor changing and means you're unlikely to meet your outfit at the same party.

Tried on properly

Come in, take your time, ask for a second opinion. We'll tell you honestly if something isn't working — that's what a boutique is for.
